Return Policy for GoldOra

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At GoldOra, customer satisfaction is our priority. We strive to provide high-quality jewellery and a seamless shopping experience. Please review our Return Policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Eligibility for Returns

You may request a return if:

The product received is damaged, defective, or incorrect.
The product is unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
The return request is submitted within 7 days of delivery.
2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return:

Customized or personalized jewellery.
Products altered, resized, repaired, or modified after delivery.
Items showing signs of wear, damage, or misuse.
Products returned without original packaging, certificates, or invoices.
3. Return Process

To initiate a return:

Contact our support team within 7 days of receiving the product.
Provide your order number and details of the issue.
If requested, share photos or videos of the product.
Once approved, instructions for return shipment will be provided.
4. Inspection & Approval

All returned products will undergo inspection upon receipt.

GoldOra reserves the right to:

Approve or reject return requests based on product condition.
Refuse returns that do not meet the eligibility requirements.
5. Refunds

If a return is approved:

Refunds will be processed to the original payment method.
Processing may take 7–10 business days after approval.
Shipping charges, if any, may be non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
6. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us immediately. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate replacement, repair, or refund.

7. Cancellation of Orders

Orders may be canceled before shipment. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation requests may not be accepted and the return process will apply instead.
