Shipping Policy for GoldOra

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

GoldOra is committed to delivering your jewellery safely and efficiently. This Shipping Policy outlines our shipping practices and timelines.

1. Order Processing
Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation.
Customized jewellery orders may require additional production time.
Orders are not processed on weekends or public holidays.
2. Shipping Coverage

We currently ship across India. Availability of shipping services may vary depending on the delivery location.

3. Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery times:

Metro Cities: 2–5 business days
Other Cities & Towns: 4–8 business days
Remote Areas: 5–10 business days

Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to factors beyond our control.

4. Shipping Charges
Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout.
Promotional offers may include free shipping on eligible orders.
5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, tracking details will be provided through the app, email, or SMS where applicable.

6. Delivery Delays

GoldOra is not responsible for delays caused by:

Courier or logistics partners
Weather conditions
Natural disasters
Government restrictions
Incorrect shipping information provided by customers
7. Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails due to incorrect address details or customer unavailability, additional delivery charges may apply for re-shipment.

8. Damaged Packages

If your package appears damaged upon delivery:

Do not accept the package if the damage is severe.
Contact our support team immediately.
Provide photos of the package and product for review.
